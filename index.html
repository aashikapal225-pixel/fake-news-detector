Fake News Detector

Paste a headline or an article. The detector marks the phrases that raise doubt in red and the ones that build trust in green.

Paste some text and select Check article. The result, the marked-up text and the reasons will appear here.

How it works

The detector reads the writing style, the way an editor would. It looks for sensational words, clickbait phrases, conspiracy framing, absolute claims, ALL-CAPS shouting, repeated exclamation marks and pressure to share.

It also rewards what honest reporting usually has: named sources, direct quotes, specific figures and measured wording. Each signal adds or removes points from a credibility score out of 100.

What it cannot do

It does not check facts. A calm, well-sourced article can still be false, and a blunt true story can score low. Treat the score as a prompt to check the source, the date and a second outlet.

Short texts give weak results, so paste at least a few sentences.
